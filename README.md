# 3-Year-Analysis-of-Gross-Margin-and-Pricing-Intervention

There has observed a significant decrease in our profit margin across the portfolio, which attribute primarily to rising Cost of Goods Sold, tariffs and other factors. Review the portfolio across the 2023-2025 order data to make recommendation in pricing strategy. <br>

Task to do:
- Identify all products with gross margin % (GMP) below 30% in Q3 2025
- Create a Dashboard Showing Year-over-Year GMP, Revenue by Category, Product and Region.
- Provide clear, data-backed recommendations on which items require a price increase or discontinuation

## Data Structure
Orders database structure consists of three tables: Orders, Products, and Customers with a total row count of 4,456 records. Orders table came from append data from order_2023, order_2024 and order_2025.<br>

## Overview of Findings
During the pricing analysis, there are 6 products that fall below 30% gross margin for the Q3 2025 which are Minimalist Keychain with (11.98%), Chemex Filters 100 packs (12.00%), Logo Hoodie Black (12.03%), Gooseneck Electric Kettle (25.52%), Branded Ceramic Mug Large (27.99%) and Pour-Over Starter Kit (28.97%). These items are producing low gross margin for the store and suggested for a newer price.<br>

In the pricing analysis based yearly, there are 3 products that needed to be addressed which are Chemex filter (16.31%), Minimalist keychain (16.35%) and Logo Hoodie Black (16.61%) needed a pricing change or discontinuation since it produces low sales margin.<br>

Demographic insights reveal a strong financial performance from 2023 to 2025 that is characterized by a total revenue of $863.70k and a healthy profit margin of 54.84%. The business is heavily driven by the "Grinders & Brewers" category, which dominates the portfolio at 58.06% ($501.49K) of total revenue, followed by "Subscriptions" at 18.87%. With a customer base of over 4,400 and 11,000 units sold, the regional data shows a balanced spread across the East, North, South, and West; however, the KPI trends highlight significant volatility and periodic spikes in activity throughout 2024 and 2025, suggesting that while hardware sales provide the core value, regional performance is subject to distinct seasonal or promotional fluctuations.<br>

## Recommendation
Based on the Gross Margin Percentage Analysis, it is advisable to immediately review the products and either discontinue or significantly raise the price by 25% or more on the following low margin accessories and merchandise:<br>
- Chemex Filters (100 packs)
- Minimalist Keychain
- Logo Hoodie (Black)
There is an item which is required for price adjustment such as Gooseneck Electric kettle to restore its gross margin percentage (GMP) above the 30% threshold. These actions are necessary because the rising Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) has made these products unprofitable or structurally weak contributors to overall profit.<br>
